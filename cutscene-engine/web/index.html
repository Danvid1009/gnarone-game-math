THE MATH

P(A) is the Live Fight rating of the pairing: an exact backward induction over power and HP. The bet is settled by one uniform against that P; odds = 0.95 ÷ P, so both sides return exactly 95%. The cutscene is drawn from a derived stream and redrawn until its winner matches, so it never changes the payout. Side bets are their own draws: coin and pick pay RTP ÷ p, single shot uses sloped bands.
