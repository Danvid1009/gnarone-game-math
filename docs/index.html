GNARONE GAME MATH

Payout engines for the Rollerz RGS. Each one has a page with its playable demo, the algorithm typeset from its ALGORITHM.md, figures, and the source. Every round is one uniform draw, fixed at bet time and shown after the round ends. RTP is exact by summation; simulation only checks the code.

Engines

GLASS BRIDGE STEPPER

A fair crash ladder. Ten rungs, cash out any time. Survival to rung k is RTP ÷ payoutₖ, so every stopping rule returns the same 97%: the player chooses variance, never expectation.

one draw, ten reveals
RED LIGHT GREEN LIGHT

A single-shot payout function. The field is the 0–10000 draw; zone widths are the probabilities; inside a zone the payout ramps from a − r to a + r. Payouts and RTP in, a monotone payout(u) out, exact by summation.
